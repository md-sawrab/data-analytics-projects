## Customer Churn Analysis

We are analyzing a **telecom provider’s churn dataset** to understand **why customers leave**.  

### What is Customer Churn?
Customer churn refers to the situation when a customer **stops using a company’s product or service** over a certain period.  

**Churn definitions may vary by context:**  
- Different industries or business models may define churn differently.  
- For example, a customer may be considered a churner if they **have not made a purchase in the past 12 months**.  

The goal of this analysis is to identify patterns and factors that influence customer churn, helping the company **retain customers and improve loyalty**.  

---

### Key Metrics (KPIs)
- **Total Customers:** 6,687  
- **Churned Customers:** 1,796  
- **Churn Rate:** 26.86%  

---

### Key Insights
- **Top Churn Drivers:** The main reasons customers churn are **Competitor Offers** and **Better Devices** provided by rivals, accounting for a **combined impact of over 70%**.  
- **Demographic Vulnerability:** **Senior citizens** exhibit the highest churn propensity at **44%**, significantly higher than other age groups.  
- **Geographic Hotspots:** **California (CA)** leads with a **75% churn rate**, followed by **Indiana (IN)** at **66.67%**.  
- **Age Trend:** There is a clear correlation between **age and churn**; customers aged **69+** are at the highest risk.  
- **Data Consumption:** Users consuming **5GB–10GB of data** are more likely to churn compared to light or heavy users.
