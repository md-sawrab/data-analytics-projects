### 📊 Sales Dashboard | Excel

#### 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and business trends. The dashboard provides key performance indicators (KPIs), sales trends, geographic insights, and category-wise analysis to support data-driven decision-making.

#### 🚀 Key Metrics

- Total Sales
- Average Rating
- Average Order Value
- Total Ratings
- Total Orders

---

#### 📊 Dashboard Features

- KPI Cards for overall business performance
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Daily Sales Trend
- Weekly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by State (Map Visualization)
- Quarterly Sales, Ratings & Orders Summary
- Top 5 Cities by Sales
- Interactive Filters (Month & City)

---

#### 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Calculated Column
- Interactive Visualizations

---

#### 📈 Business Insights

This dashboard helps answer key business questions such as:

- How do sales perform over time?
- Which states generate the highest revenue?
- Which cities contribute the most sales?
- How do different product categories compare?
- Which quarter performs best in terms of sales and orders?
- What are the overall customer rating trends?
